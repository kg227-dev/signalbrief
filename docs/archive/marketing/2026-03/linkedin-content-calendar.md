# SignalBrief — LinkedIn Content Calendar
## 90 Days of Posts, Written and Ready

*Post 3x/week: Monday, Wednesday, Friday. Best window: 7:00–8:30 AM ET.*
*Each post ends with a CTA to getsignalbrief.com — vary the wording so it doesn't feel robotic.*
*Tone: smart, specific, no hype. You're a strategy professional sharing what you know.*

---

## HOW TO USE THIS CALENDAR

1. Each week, pick the posts for that week and pull the insight from that morning's actual digest
2. Replace `[SIGNAL]` and `[ANALYSIS]` with real content from SignalBrief — this makes every post authentic
3. Add 3–5 relevant hashtags: `#strategy #consulting #privateequity #healthcare [sector]`
4. If a post does well (50+ likes or 5+ comments), boost it with a follow-up reply 24h later

## CONTENT RULES

1. **Never lead with the product.** Lead with the insight, the pain, or the take. The product is the conclusion.
2. **No emojis.** Your audience is strategy professionals. They'll notice.
3. **No hashtag walls.** Max 3 hashtags, relevant only.
4. **Respond to every comment within 2 hours.** The algorithm rewards engagement, and a comment asking "how does this compare to X" is your best free marketing.
5. **Repost your best performers after 30 days.** With a new intro line. Different timezone audience sees it fresh.
6. **If a post hits 100+ likes, pin it to your profile.** It becomes your social proof.
