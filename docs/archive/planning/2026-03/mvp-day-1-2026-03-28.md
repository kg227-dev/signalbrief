### Hypothesis: what's driving Day 1 results

1. **The pipeline works.** Selection, scoring, freshness, dedup, lane classification — all functioning correctly. 5 topics delivered strong results without intervention.
2. **Source coverage is the binding constraint.** Every problem traces back to source availability.
3. **The scoring model over-values source authority.** Wired coupon pages outscore legitimate news from lower-tier sources.
4. **Source cap of 2 is tight for topics with 1-2 dominant sources.** Financial Services is essentially an americanbanker.com brief.
