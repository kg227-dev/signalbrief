**Day 10 is the worst delivery day in the validation run.** 24 of 35 items delivered, 11 underfills driven by writeup failure, 5 of 7 topics with sub-15 candidate depth, and trusted share collapsed to 2/24 (8.3%). The root cause is not primarily writeup failure — it is a fetch-level pool collapse. Consumer & Retail had 3 candidates, Life Sciences had 2. No writeup fix can fill a 5-item topic from a 2-item pool.

### Hypothesis: what's driving Day 10 results

1. **The fetch layer had a pool collapse on Sunday.**
   100 total candidates from a normal weekday pool of 200+. Five of seven topics dropped below depth-15. The most likely causes are reduced Sunday publisher volume, a broker feed interruption, or a scheduling gap in discovery. This needs to be verified against the scheduler and fetch logs before attributing it to editorial or selection logic.

2. **`provider_parse_failure` is still not fixed.**
   14 drops today, same root cause as Day 8 and Day 9. The repair improvement (6/9) partially offsets this, but the first-pass failure rate (23/41 = 56%) means the validator is still rejecting more than half of writeup attempts on the first try.

3. **The selection layer is not routing the right candidates to writeup first.**
   The americanbanker.com score=1.0 story was not selected while 8 lower-quality Financial Services candidates went through writeup. This suggests the selector is not strictly priority-ordering by score before feeding the writeup queue, or there is a pool-routing bug causing the top item to be passed over.

4. **Life Sciences and Consumer & Retail need fetch-level fixes, not writeup fixes.**
   A topic with 2 or 3 candidates cannot be repaired by tuning the validator. The broker coverage or discovery configuration for these topics is insufficient.
