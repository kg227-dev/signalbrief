### Hypothesis: what's driving Day 11 results

1. **Monday 72h window is doing the right thing.**
   The pool was thin on Sunday and full on Monday because the same content was within range. Saturday needs the same treatment — extend to 72h on weekends and Monday, not just Monday.

2. **`provider_parse_failure` was content-quality driven, not a systematic prompt bug.**
   Zero parse failures today on a 351-candidate pool with 41 writeup attempts. The failures on Days 8–10 correlated exactly with the Sunday/weekend pool — shorter summaries, thinner article previews from weekend-published content, or stale content that parse-failed for structural reasons. This is important: the fix may not require prompt changes, but the raw response logging from `3800f54` will confirm.

3. **The WSJ/pymnts fix matters most on thin-pool days.**
   On a full Monday, Financial Services had 40 candidates and didn't need WSJ. On a Sunday with 13 candidates, those 20 WSJ articles could have saved the topic. The fix is most impactful on weekends — confirm it's deployed before the next Sunday run (2026-04-12).

4. **Source concentration is now the quality ceiling.**
   With pool depth and writeup reliability largely recovered, the remaining quality gap is per-source capping. Financial Services got 5 americanbanker items (all good), but missed the Takeda/Denali story and the AI-priority banker survey. A per-source cap of 3 would force more diversity — but that's a tuning decision, not a bug.
