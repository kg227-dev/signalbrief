**Day 12 is mechanically healthy, but not exit-green.** The run delivered 35/35 items, 7/7 topics full, 7/7 topics above depth-15, 568 total candidates, 58/58 broker source fetches successful, and 0 underfills due to writeup. That is the operational floor we need.

**The quality regression is real.** Trusted T1/2 share fell to 22/35 (62.9%) from Day 11's 26/35 (74.3%), still below the 80% MVP target. The selected set contains 22 strong items and 13 standard items. There are no selected unknown-tier or official filler items, so this is a different failure from Days 8-10: the scoring and reserve ordering are still letting standard-tier items beat available strong-tier candidates on adequate-depth days.

**Writeup reliability also regressed.** The run attempted 53 writeups: 21 first-pass successes, 22 repair attempts, 14 repair successes, and 18 drops. Repair is still useful at 63.6%, but `provider_parse_failure` returned with 10 drops after being absent on Day 11. The biggest casualties were strong Financial Services and Healthcare items from americanbanker.com, modernhealthcare.com, statnews.com, and bankingdive.com.

### What worked

   Every topic cleared depth-15. The pool was 568 candidates after story relationship filtering, and the final lane breakdown was entirely broker-fed: 534 publisher-feed candidates and 34 official candidates.

2. **The source fetch path stayed healthy.**
   The broker attempted 58 sources and all 58 succeeded. There is no Day 12 evidence of a transport outage or scheduler failure.

3. **The WSJ/pymnts non-article failure appears fixed.**
   `financial_wsj_markets` parsed 20 items with `non_article_count=0`, and `consumer_pymnts_retail` parsed 10 items with `non_article_count=0`. On Day 11 both were silently dropped as non-article. The remaining issue is that all retained candidates from both sources were stale today: WSJ `retained=0, stale=20`; pymnts `retained=0, stale=10`.

   Source-cap rejections are visible for americanbanker.com, modernhealthcare.com, and retaildive.com. This prevented single-source monopolies in Financial Services, Healthcare, and Consumer & Retail without causing underfill.

5. **Financial Services and Healthcare ended clean despite writeup drops.**
   Both topics finished 5/5 strong-tier. The writeup gate dropped better first-choice items, but the reserve pool was deep enough to keep final quality acceptable.

### Hypothesis: what's driving Day 12 results

1. **Fetch and source availability are no longer the blocker.**
   The run had enough candidates, full source success, and no discovery dependence. The remaining issues are selection ordering, strategic relevance, and writeup reliability.

2. **The writeup gate is intermittently failing on good sources.**
   Day 12 invalidates the clean Day 11 hypothesis that parse failure was only weekend/thin-content related. Use the raw-response logging to inspect the failed americanbanker.com, modernhealthcare.com, statnews.com, and bankingdive.com responses.

3. **The selection layer needs a trusted-first floor on adequate-depth days.**
   Unknown-tier backfill is no longer selected, but standard-tier items are still filling too much of Technology, Life Sciences, Energy, and Industrials even when enough premium/strong candidates exist. The reserve queue should prefer T1/2 candidates before standard candidates when a topic has sufficient trusted inventory.

4. **Technology needs a harder strategic relevance gate.**
   Personal device experiences, social-app culture, and lightweight product launches should not beat AI security, platform policy, enterprise infrastructure, or capital-allocation stories.

### Fix plan

1. **Parse-failure remediation first.**
   Pull the raw model responses for the 10 Day 12 `provider_parse_failure` items and classify them as malformed JSON, empty provider output, truncation, or validator mismatch. If malformed, force stricter structured output and make repair retry parse-specific. If empty/thin, pass richer source evidence before writeup.

2. **Add a topic-level trusted floor.**
   On topics with at least five T1/2 candidates after freshness/history filters, select or backfill at least four T1/2 items before allowing standard-tier reserves, unless all trusted candidates fail writeup. Emit an audit reason when the floor is bypassed.

3. **Change reserve ordering.**
   `writeup_failed` backfill should draw from same-topic premium/strong candidates before standard candidates on adequate-depth days. This would directly address Life Sciences and Industrials without blocking standard fill on truly thin days.

4. **Tighten Technology relevance.**
   Add negative signals for personal device reviews, app-feature-only stories, culture/meta commentary, and non-operator consumer tech. Add positive signals for AI security, platform governance, enterprise infrastructure, compute, regulatory decisions, and capital allocation.

5. **Monitor WSJ/pymnts for one more weekday.**
   The non-article bug appears fixed, but both feeds were fully stale on Day 12. If that repeats on a normal publish day, inspect feed dates and URL parsing; otherwise treat them as useful only when recent content exists.
