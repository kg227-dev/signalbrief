### What worked

1. **The system recovered from Day 13's validator wall without losing delivery depth.**
   Day 14 still delivered 35/35, all seven topics remained at 5/5, and total writeup drops fell from 143 to 15 while broker retrieval stayed fully healthy.

2. **Trusted share finally reached the single-day expansion target.**
   The final set landed at 28/35 trusted T1/T2 items (80.0%), with five topics at 4/5 or better trusted and no topic at 0/5 trusted.

3. **Industrials and Consumer & Retail both moved out of structural-failure territory.**
   Industrials improved from 0/5 trusted on Day 13 to 3/5 trusted, and Consumer & Retail improved from 1/5 trusted to 3/5 trusted. Those are still the weakest topics, but they are no longer catastrophic.

4. **Life Sciences stayed clean while Technology remained directionally better than Day 12.**
   Life Sciences finished 4/5 trusted with zero writeup drops. Technology held 4/5 trusted with operator-relevant trade coverage, which is a materially healthier outcome than the consumer-noise mix seen on Day 12.

### What failed

1. **First-pass writeup generation is still weak.**
   Only 5 of 50 writeups passed on the first attempt (10.0%). The run depended on a perfect 30/30 repair pass to stay healthy, which is not a stable production posture.

2. **The strong-tier drop rate is still above the expansion bar.**
   Day 14 dropped 9 of 37 strong-tier writeups (24.3%). That is much better than Day 13, but still too high for confident category expansion.

3. **Consumer & Retail remains the clearest weak topic.**
   The topic finished only 3/5 trusted, needed 11 writeup attempts, dropped 6 writeups (54.5%), and still lost one of four strong-tier attempts. It is the largest remaining category-specific blocker.

4. **The audit has a summary-aggregation mismatch that should be fixed before relying on the top-line counters.**
   The top-level `summary.writeup` block records `soft_fail_count: 0` and `minimum_viable_accept_count: 0`, but the per-topic writeup blocks sum to 28 soft-fail recoveries and 7 minimum-viable accepts. The candidate-level statuses show 30 `repair_pass`, 5 `model_pass`, and 15 `failed_dropped`, so the per-topic numbers look more credible than the summary aggregate.

### Hypothesis: what is driving Day 14

1. **The post-`f95130c83e7733a43a4c7dc011021d46f1cc02d2` validator changes did stabilize the pipeline.**
   Relative to Day 13, the system is no longer losing most of the strong pool at writeup time.

2. **The remaining blocker is now narrower and more tractable.**
   The current miss pattern is concentrated in concision enforcement on otherwise viable writeups, especially in Consumer & Retail and parts of Technology.

3. **Trusted-first backfill is better, but not fully solved.**
   The fact that Consumer & Retail and Industrials are still only 3/5 trusted, while `standard_tier_blocked_while_strong_available` remains true in every topic bucket, suggests the selector/backfill policy is still willing to settle too early once a few strong candidates drop.

### Fix plan

1. **Tune the writeup concision validator first.**
   Sample the 15 dropped Day 14 writeups, especially Consumer & Retail and Technology, and loosen the checks around `what_happened_not_concise` and `mechanism_not_concise` where the candidate is otherwise strong and specific.

2. **Audit trusted-first backfill in weak lanes.**
   Consumer & Retail and Industrials should not stop at 3/5 trusted when the topic bucket still reports strong availability. Force same-topic trusted recovery ahead of standard fallthrough.

3. **Fix audit aggregation parity.**
   Reconcile `summary.writeup` against per-topic writeup counters so `soft_fail_count` and `minimum_viable_accept_count` are trustworthy in the operator summary.

4. **Run one more live day before expanding categories.**
   Expansion should stay blocked until trusted share holds >=75% again, writeup drops stay comfortably below 30%, strong-tier drop rate falls to <=15%, and Consumer & Retail proves it can hold 4/5 or 5/5 trusted.
