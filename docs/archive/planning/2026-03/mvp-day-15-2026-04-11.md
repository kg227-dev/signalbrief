Strong-tier writeups were attempted 39 times, 26 survived into the final set, and 13 dropped for a **33.3% strong-tier drop rate**, which is materially worse than Day 14's 24.3% and still far above the <=15% expansion bar. All 13 parse failures were still classified as `validator_mismatch`.

**Judgment:** the product is still **not on track for expansion**. Day 15 met the writeup-drop-share threshold and maintained 5/5 fulfillment in every topic, but trusted share slipped back under the consistency bar, strong-tier drop rate worsened, and two lanes still look structurally weak at only 2/5 trusted. The blocker has narrowed to writeup/validator survivability and same-topic trusted recovery, not retrieval volume.

### What worked

1. **The runtime fix restored same-day delivery.**
   The morning run was blocked by stale breaker state, but after the runtime patch the day was replayed, the archive and audit were generated, and the recovery send completed successfully for all 10 canary recipients.

2. **Retrieval depth remained healthy despite the delivery incident.**
   Every topic still cleared depth-15, total retained candidates landed at 365, and the pool stayed almost entirely broker-fed with zero discovery dependence.

### What failed

1. **The parser/validator layer is still the main content-quality blocker.**
   Day 15's run diagnosis labeled the day `STATUS: RED` with `PRIMARY: PARSE OR STRUCTURED OUTPUT FAILURE`. The top three machine diagnoses were `parse_or_structured_output_failure`, `validator_over_reject`, and `selection_ranking_failure`.

2. **Strong-tier loss is still too high for launch.**
   The run dropped 13 of 39 strong-tier writeup attempts (33.3%). That is a regression from Day 14 and far above the <=15% target needed before category expansion.

3. **Industrials and Consumer & Retail remain weak-topic blockers.**
   Both lanes finished only 2/5 trusted. Industrials filled five slots but leaned on FreightWaves standard items and an unknown-tier Area Development item. Consumer & Retail again backfilled into a mixed standard-tier set after strong candidates failed writeup.

4. **The top-level audit summary is still undercounting soft-fail recovery.**
   `summary.writeup` reports `soft_fail_count: 0` and `minimum_viable_accept_count: 0`, but the per-topic writeup blocks sum to **31 soft fails recovered** and **9 minimum-viable accepts**. The day note should continue to trust the topic-level counters over the top-line aggregate.

### Hypothesis: what is driving Day 15

1. **The runtime/delivery incident and the content-quality blocker were separate issues.**
   The morning miss came from stale breaker state, which is now fixed. The audit still shows the content pipeline itself is blocked by parse/validator survivability rather than retrieval depth.

2. **The writeup contract remains too brittle for strong-tier candidates.**
   Day 15 kept the narrow `validator_mismatch` failure shape, dropped 13 strong candidates, and required repair on 27 writeups to hold the day together.

3. **Backfill still settles too early in weak lanes.**
   Industrials and Consumer & Retail did not underfill, but both still ended at 2/5 trusted. That means same-topic trusted recovery remains too weak after writeup loss.

4. **The rolling MVP window is still below launch quality even with stable broker retrieval.**
   The admin readiness panel still shows 63.1% trusted share and 247 missed-story flags over the 15-day window. Retrieval backbone reliability is no longer the limiting factor.

### Fix plan

1. **Fix structured-output parsing and malformed-response recovery first.**
   Day 15's machine diagnosis and the 13 `validator_mismatch` drops both point to the same blocker. This is still the first thing to fix before touching retrieval expansion.

2. **Relax validator over-reject behavior via soft-fail acceptance, not broader retrieval.**
   The topic-level writeup blocks show 31 soft-fail recoveries and 9 minimum-viable accepts that the summary layer hides. Preserve more of these candidates before dropping to weak same-topic backfill.

3. **Improve trusted same-topic backfill in Industrials and Consumer & Retail.**
   Those lanes should not keep ending at 2/5 trusted on healthy-depth days.

4. **Fix audit summary parity.**
   The top-level writeup counters need to match the per-topic blocks before operators can trust the dashboard summary without drilling into each lane.
