### What worked

1. **Validator calibration clearly worked on Day 16.**
   There were no dropped writeups, no strong-tier losses, no hard fails, and no parse failures. First-pass success jumped from 10.0% on Day 14 and 16.7% on Day 15 to 54.3% on Day 16.

2. **Fulfillment and retrieval depth stayed stable.**
   The run still delivered 35/35 items, all seven topics stayed full, and every topic held at depth-15 or better even as the total retained pool compressed from 601 on Day 14 to 365 on Day 15 to 284 on Day 16.

3. **Financial Services stayed clean and the mid-tier strong topics held.**
   Financial Services finished 5/5 trusted, while Technology, Healthcare, Life Sciences, and Energy all stayed at 4/5 trusted with zero writeup drops.

4. **The blocker is now narrower and easier to name.**
   The run diagnosis flipped from `parse_or_structured_output_failure` on Day 14 and Day 15 to `selection_ranking_failure` on Day 16, with `low_trust_selection_mix` as the only secondary issue.

### What failed

1. **Trusted share regressed sharply once validator losses disappeared.**
   Day 16 dropped to 23/35 trusted (65.7%), which is 11.4 points below Day 15 and 14.3 points below Day 14. That regression is why the three-run window finished below the >=75% consistency gate.

2. **Industrials became a structural blocker again.**
   Industrials finished **0/5 trusted** despite clearing the depth-15 floor. The final set was three FreightWaves items plus two Area Development fills, and the topic diagnosis explicitly called out `trusted_pool_available_but_not_selected`.

3. **Consumer & Retail remained expansion-blocking even with zero writeup loss.**
   The lane still ended only **2/5 trusted**. This is the clearest evidence that weak-topic outcomes are now dominated by ranking and source-mix decisions rather than validator drops.

4. **Minimum-viable acceptance is now extremely high.**
   The run shipped **28 minimum-viable accepts** and recovered **29 soft fails**. That kept delivery intact, but it also means the writeup layer is no longer filtering much; the selector is effectively choosing among many candidates that only barely clear the shipping bar.

### Hypothesis: what is driving Day 16

1. **The calibration window proved the validator fix.**
   Day 16 eliminated the `validator_mismatch` parse-failure shape entirely and preserved every strong-tier writeup attempt.

2. **Ranking is now the clear primary blocker.**
   The run diagnosis switched to `selection_ranking_failure`, and six of seven topic buckets still reported `standard_tier_blocked_while_strong_available`.

3. **Trusted-first same-topic backfill is still too weak in the worst lanes.**
   Industrials fell from 3/5 trusted on Day 14 to 2/5 on Day 15 to 0/5 on Day 16. Consumer & Retail stayed stuck at 2/5 for a second straight day.

4. **The validator may now be slightly too permissive, but that is not the first fix.**
   Shipping 28 minimum-viable accepts in a 35-item day is probably too high for steady-state quality, but the larger user-visible failure is still that low-trust items are winning final selection when better supply exists.

### Fix plan

1. **Move the next fix to selector-side trusted-first ranking and reserve ordering.**
   Day 16 is the strongest evidence yet that validator work should stop being first in the queue.

2. **Fix Industrials first, then Consumer & Retail.**
   Industrials is now a 0/5-trusted hard blocker; Consumer & Retail is the second lane that still cannot clear the trust bar even with zero writeup loss.

3. **Use minimum-viable accepts as a secondary cleanup item after ranking.**
   Once the selector stops preferring low-trust baskets, tighten the minimum-viable floor so 28/35 accepted borderline writeups does not become the new normal.

4. **Keep category expansion blocked.**
   The three-run window did not satisfy the trusted-share consistency gate and still contains a structurally weak topic, so expansion should remain paused until a new selector fix is validated.
