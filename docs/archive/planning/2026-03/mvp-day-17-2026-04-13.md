   The top-line diagnosis shifted to **`retrieval_thinness`** with **`selection_ranking_failure`** secondary. That is more actionable than the earlier validator wall because it points directly at topic supply and source-pack weakness.

   Consumer & Retail had only **3 total candidates**, all from **modernretail.co**, and finished **3/5** with **0/3 trusted**. That alone means the run is not launch-safe.

   Industrials again shipped **0/5 trusted**, and this time it did so on just **6 candidates**, which means the lane is now both thin and low-trust.

4. **Four topics were retrieval-thin.**
   Healthcare, Life Sciences, Industrials, and Consumer & Retail all finished below the depth-15 floor. The run-level evidence shows **4 thin topics**, **4 missed-story flags**, and only **3/7 depth-healthy topics**.

### Hypothesis: what is driving Day 17

1. **The validator calibration held.**
   Day 17 preserved the Day 16 writeup behavior: no drops, no strong-tier losses, and no parse failures.

2. **Retrieval thinness widened beyond the weak-topic pair.**
   The run-level diagnosis elevated `retrieval_thinness` to the primary root cause because four topics fell below depth-15 and one of them underfilled.

3. **Trusted-first ranking is still weak in the healthy lanes.**
   Technology and Energy still reported `trusted_pool_available_but_not_selected`, and Financial Services regressed into a clearly low-trust final mix.

4. **Minimum-viable acceptance is masking supply weakness.**
   The run still shipped **26 minimum-viable accepts** out of 33 selected items. That kept output volume from collapsing further, but it did not improve trust quality.

### Fix plan

1. **Fix retrieval supply for Consumer & Retail first.**
   The lane underfilled and had only three total candidates, so it is the most acute launch blocker.

2. **Fix retrieval supply for Industrials, Life Sciences, and Healthcare next.**
   Those lanes all failed the depth-15 floor, and Industrials is still a 0/5-trusted blocker.

3. **Then retune trusted-first ranking in Financial Services, Technology, and Energy.**
   Those lanes had enough supply to fill, but still left trusted outcomes on the table or leaned too hard on low-trust domains.

4. **Keep category expansion blocked.**
   Day 17 failed the trusted-share bar, the strong category-health bar, and the stable 5/5 fulfillment bar. Expansion should remain paused until thin-topic supply is repaired and the selector can sustain >=75% trusted share again.
