### What worked

1. **Retrieval depth and fulfillment fully recovered.**
   Day 18 returned to **35/35 delivered**, **7/7 depth-healthy topics**, and a **350-candidate** retained pool after Day 17 fell to **33/35** and only **3/7** depth-healthy topics.

2. **Trusted share rebounded above the expansion target.**
   The final set landed at **30/35 trusted (85.7%)**, beating Day 14's 80.0% and far above the Day 14-16 calibration average of 73.3%.

3. **Most topics are now individually launch-shaped.**
   Financial Services, Healthcare, Life Sciences, and Energy all finished **5/5 trusted**. Technology and Consumer & Retail both landed **4/5 trusted**.

4. **Writeup stayed strong enough that it is no longer the dominant blocker.**
   The run dropped only **2/37** writeups and only **1/31** strong-tier attempts, which is materially better than Day 14 and Day 15 and still acceptable even with the validator mismatch relapse.

### What failed

1. **Validator mismatch reappeared and is the run-level primary diagnosis.**
   The audit labeled Day 18 **`parse_or_structured_output_failure`** primary because **2 writeups** dropped on **`validator_mismatch`**, producing **2 hard fails** and breaking the zero-drop posture seen on Day 16 and Day 17.

2. **Industrials is still a structural no-go topic.**
   Industrials delivered **5/5** with **22 candidates**, but only **2/5** final selections were trusted and all five shipped as minimum-viable accepts. That alone keeps category expansion blocked under the "no structurally weak topics" rule.

3. **Ranking still leaves trusted supply on the table in most lanes.**
   Six of seven topic diagnoses reported **`trusted_pool_available_but_not_selected`**, which means the selector is still settling for weaker shapes even when stronger inventory exists.

4. **Minimum-viable acceptance remains too high for steady-state confidence.**
   The run shipped **30 minimum-viable accepts** out of 35 selected items. That is workable during tuning, but it means the final baskets are still leaning heavily on permissive recovery rather than clearly strong writeups.

### Hypothesis: what is driving Day 18

1. **The system can still produce expansion-quality trust on a single day.**
   Day 18's **85.7% trusted share** shows the source packs are good enough across most lanes when retrieval depth is healthy.

2. **The writeup layer is still slightly unstable, but no longer the whole story.**
   Two `validator_mismatch` drops are enough to keep parse failure as the formal top-line diagnosis, yet they are too small to explain a lane like Industrials landing at **2/5 trusted** with zero topic-level drops.

3. **Ranking is now the main user-visible quality miss.**
   The per-topic diagnoses are overwhelmingly **`trusted_pool_available_but_not_selected`**, and the weakest lane is weak because trusted candidates are not winning, not because supply is absent.

4. **Industrials remains the gating proof point.**
   Until Industrials can hold **4/5 or 5/5 trusted** on a day with depth and zero writeup loss, the product is not ready to expand categories even if the top-line trust rate looks strong elsewhere.

### Fix plan

1. **Harden structured-output parsing and validator softness first.**
   Eliminate the residual `validator_mismatch` drops so strong candidates are no longer lost in Financial Services and Energy.

2. **Retune trusted-first ranking in Industrials immediately after.**
   Industrials is the only still-structural blocker in this run, and it failed despite sufficient depth and zero writeup loss.

3. **Then clean up trusted-pool selection in Technology and Consumer & Retail.**
   Both lanes were directionally healthy, but the topic diagnoses still show weaker story shapes beating trusted supply.

4. **Keep category expansion blocked.**
   Day 18 is a good rebound day, not a release gate. Expansion should stay paused until trusted share stays >=75% across consecutive live days and Industrials stops shipping as a low-trust lane.
