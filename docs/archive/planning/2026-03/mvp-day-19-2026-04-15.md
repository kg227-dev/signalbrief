### Hypothesis: what is driving Day 19

1. **The post-commit validator path is now good enough for launch.**
   Day 19 shows the writeup layer can complete a full run with **0 drops**, **0 parse failures**, and **100% repair success**.

2. **Trusted supply is present across the board.**
   Every topic was depth-healthy, every topic had missed-story flags, and every topic diagnosis called out **`trusted_pool_available_but_not_selected`**.

3. **Pool-cut and source-cap behavior are still choosing the wrong stories in the marginal slots.**
   The weakest final picks in Industrials, Technology, and Life Sciences were not forced by thin supply. They were chosen while stronger alternatives were available.

4. **The calibration window is trending well, but not complete.**
   Two consecutive runs now meet the top-line trust and drop-rate thresholds. The remaining proof point is whether the third live run can hold those metrics without a relapse in the weak topics.

### Fix plan

1. **Retune ranking and pool-cut behavior in Industrials first.**
   This is still the weakest topic and the clearest expansion blocker.

2. **Then fix topic relevance and trusted-first ordering in Technology and Life Sciences.**
   Both lanes had ample supply and still admitted avoidable weaker shapes.

3. **Then tighten source-cap fallback behavior in Energy and Consumer & Retail.**
   Those lanes are healthy, but they still leave obvious trusted stories behind when a dominant source hits its cap.

4. **Keep category expansion blocked until Day 20 closes the calibration window cleanly.**
   The trajectory is now good enough to call "on track," but not yet good enough to unlock expansion before the third live confirmation run.
