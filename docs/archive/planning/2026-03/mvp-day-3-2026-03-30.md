### Hypothesis: what's driving Day 3 results

1. **The 07:00 ET Monday run captures mostly weekend content.** 48h lookback at 07:02 ET Monday covers Saturday 07:00 → Monday 07:00. Most weekday trade publishing happens after 07:00 ET (mid-morning embargoes, market-open coverage).
2. **Financial Services' Monday failure is more serious than a weekend gap.** americanbanker.com items appear in Technology pool, proving the source is fetched. FinServ got 0 candidates post-dedup — likely all Day 2 duplicates or over-reliance on federalregister.gov.
3. **Consumer & Retail's thin Monday pool points to a source depth problem, not a timing problem.**
4. **Sponsored content / white papers are polluting Healthcare's candidate pool.** 3 of 7 Healthcare candidates are modernhealthcare.com sponsored content scoring 0.881.
5. **Technology topic selection is leaking non-tech content.** STAT News public health obituary was *selected* as a Technology item.
