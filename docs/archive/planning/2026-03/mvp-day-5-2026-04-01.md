### Hypothesis: what's driving Day 5 results

1. **Trusted share is now structurally limited by tier classification, not source caps or contamination.** FinServ/Healthcare/Life Sciences hit 100% trusted. The 77.1% average is entirely driven by Energy (40%) and Industrials (40%) where the best-in-class sources are standard-tier. This won't be fixed by further cap or guard changes — it requires either promoting canarymedia/freightwaves to strong-tier or adding more strong-tier sources to those topics.
2. **FDA official sources are producing noise in Life Sciences.** The FDA compliance list items score well on domain authority but are not news. A `title_exclude_patterns` entry on the `life_sciences_fda_drug_labels` or similar source, or a content-type filter, would help.
3. **FreightWaves source ordering picks lower-quality items first.** The CBP smuggling story scored 0.894 (freshest item, published 0.1h ago) and won by freshness. The two cap-blocked stories about CN Rail and the Navy shipyard are better Industrials content. This is a scoring balance issue — freshness bonus overweights very-new items over more topically relevant ones.
4. **arstechnica.com should be added to DOMAIN_TOPIC_SCOPE for TECHNOLOGY only** — prevents it from appearing in Energy/other topic pools.
