### Hypothesis: what's driving Day 6 results

1. **The system is now operationally reliable enough that ranking defects are fully exposed.** Day 6 is no longer about thin pools or source outages. It is about what wins inside a healthy pool.
2. **Authority is still overpowering content shape.** FDA and other official sources can still win even when they are records pages, safety notices, or non-sector content.
3. **The topic-scope guard is incomplete.** Cross-topic contamination has narrowed, but official domains and broad tech/business sources can still leak into the wrong sector.
4. **The new writeup validation/repair work likely introduced a scheduled-path persistence problem.** The sent record payloads show zero retained `wim` / `wim_brief`, so either the validator is nulling too aggressively or the enriched fields are being lost between selection, persistence, and delivery.
