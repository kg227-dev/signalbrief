**Operationally, Day 8 stayed strong: 490 candidates, 35 selected, 7/7 topics at 5 items, 10/10 canaries delivered, 0 freshness breaches, 0 duplicate URLs versus Day 7, 100% broker backbone, and 58/58 source fetches successful.**

**The major Day 8 win is real writeup persistence recovery.** The production audit now stores `35/35` selected items with `writeup_status=model_pass` and `writeup_version=v2`, and the canary records show model-generated writeup metadata on all `80/80` delivered item instances. ### What worked

1. **The scheduled `why it matters` path recovered.**
   Day 8 is the first live day where the stored scheduled records actually contain real v2 writeup fields again instead of `wim=null` / `wim_brief=null`.

2. **The mechanical contract stayed clean.**
   All 10 canaries delivered. All 7 topics stayed at `5/5`. Every topic cleared `>=15` candidates. No resend, regenerate, or rerun was needed.

3. **The v2 drop-and-backfill loop worked mechanically.**
   Strict-quality tracking shows `0` underfills due to writeup failure even though the system dropped `63` attempted writeups. Backfill attempts ranged from `6` to `12` per topic and still preserved the count contract.

4. **Fetch health and backbone quality remain strong.**
   The run stayed 100% broker-fed and 100% source-successful. There is no Day 8 evidence of scheduler, freshness, or discovery-lane instability.

### What failed

1. **Trusted share collapsed.**
   The selected set was only `5 premium + 6 strong + 12 standard + 12 unknown`, or `31.4%` trusted T1/2. That is a severe regression from Day 7's `80.0%`.

2. **Life Sciences regressed to pure FDA.**
   All 5 selected Life Sciences items came from `fda.gov`, including `Generic Drug User Fee Amendments`, `Drug Safety and Availability`, and `FDA's Labeling Resources for Human Prescription Drugs`. This happened even though the pool still contained retained inventory from FierceBiotech, FiercePharma, Endpoints, Biopharma Dive, and STAT.

3. **The writeup validator is too brittle.**
   Of `98` writeup attempts, `63` failed and only `35` passed on the first try. `26` went to repair and `0` recovered. The dominant rejection reason was `provider_parse_failure` (`36` cases), followed by `invalid_implication_type` (`26`) and `brief_too_long` (`26`).

4. **Several selected topics are still not briefing-grade despite being full.**
   - Healthcare selected 5 Becker's items, including executive-moves and naming-rights content.
   - Consumer & Retail selected menu, brand-ambition, and restaurant novelty items.
   - Energy selected MarketWatch housing and stock-market stories alongside only one clearly on-topic utility item.
   - Financial Services still selected a Federal Register closed-meetings notice and a retail-investor MarketWatch advice story.

### Hypothesis: what's driving Day 8 results

1. **The writeup path is alive again, but the validator is now the dominant failure mode.**
   The product is no longer failing because it cannot persist analysis. It is failing because too many strong stories die inside the analysis gate.

2. **Backfill is preserving mechanical success by accepting lower-quality survivors.**
   Because the repair pass recovered `0` items, the selector kept walking same-topic reserves until it found stories that passed the gate, even if those stories were lower-trust or lower-signal.

3. **Official and low-trust winners are still beating better trade reporting once writeup failures start.**
   That is why Life Sciences, Energy, Financial Services, and Consumer & Retail all stayed full while still feeling less strategic than the product promise.
