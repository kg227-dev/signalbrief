# Marketing Strategy

*Last reviewed: March 13, 2026*

This is the canonical marketing strategy doc. Source materials from the March 2026 planning burst are archived under [`docs/archive/marketing/2026-03/`](../archive/marketing/2026-03/README.md).

## Audience

Primary audience:

- junior-to-mid strategy consultants
- corporate strategy and corp-dev associates
- multi-sector operators who need fast morning situational awareness

These users have the same underlying problem: they cover multiple sectors, need to sound informed early in the day, and do not want to maintain a six-newsletter reading habit.

## Positioning

SignalBrief is the personalized morning briefing for people who work across sectors professionally.

Core promise:

- one briefing instead of a scattered reading stack
- multi-sector coverage instead of siloed newsletters
- consultant-grade "why it matters" framing instead of consumer-grade summaries

Competitive angle:

- Morning Brew and similar products are broad but not personalized.
- Axios-style products are concise but beat-specific.
- AI summarizers save time but usually do not deliver analyst-grade implications.

The positioning line to protect:

> SignalBrief tells you what matters for your sectors, not what happened for everyone.

## Product-Led Growth Priorities

Use the current product strengths in messaging:

- immediate proof of value through the current signup and digest experience
- public digest pages as demos of the product in action
- custom topics and feedback loops as evidence of real personalization

Near-term site and message priorities:

1. Homepage clarity
   Replace brand-first language with use-case-first language.
2. Conversion trust
   Add real testimonials or company proof only when genuine evidence exists.
3. Search capture
   Publish and index consultant-specific and sector-specific content.
4. Referral loop
   Make sharing and colleague referral easier from the product surface.

## SEO And Content Priorities

The SEO opportunity is niche specificity, not volume.

Current focus:

- keyword-rich homepage H1, title, and meta description
- FAQ content plus `FAQPage` schema
- explicit indexing posture for public digest pages
- a small number of high-intent pages rather than a large generic blog

Priority content themes:

- morning briefing for strategy professionals
- consultant newsletter alternatives
- staying current across multiple sectors
- public digest pages that prove the product output

## KPI Definitions

Use a small stable set of metrics:

- active subscribers
- weekly new signups
- 7-day open rate
- digest-two open rate or equivalent early-retention proxy
- referral-driven signups

Interpretation rules:

- open rate is a quality and habit metric, not a vanity metric
- new signups matter only if digest-two retention is healthy
- referrals are the strongest sign that the product is earning a place in a user's workflow

## 30-60-90 Direction

### Days 0-30

- sharpen homepage messaging
- run direct outreach and collect real user feedback
- make public digest pages usable as acquisition assets

### Days 31-60

- add social proof once it is real
- strengthen referral prompts and sharing UX
- publish a small number of search-targeted pages with clear signup paths

### Days 61-90

- double down on channels that produce retained readers, not just clicks
- expand the referral and community loop only after activation quality is solid
- avoid broad top-of-funnel expansion until the morning habit is strong

## Non-Goals

- broad consumer-news positioning
- high-volume content production without clear conversion intent
- paid acquisition before the core retention loop improves
