How This Page Works

This page answers one question: did SignalBrief find good articles, and did it keep the right ones?
The pipeline
1. Raw retrieval
Perplexity returns candidate articles.
2. Cleanup
We remove stale items, duplicates, weak fits, and policy problems.
3. Final selection
We keep the strongest items for the scenario or persona.

What The Main Terms Mean
  • Raw: what Perplexity returned.
  • Cleaned: what survived freshness, dedupe, and source-policy cleanup.
  • Final: what SignalBrief kept.
  • Lift: whether selection helped or hurt versus the raw baseline.
  • 429: provider rate-limit errors.
  • Fail closed: we preferred showing less rather than padding with noisy filler.
