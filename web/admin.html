How to read this
1. Did the digest go out?
Users tells you how many people actually got that run. Sent to shows who they were. Total is what that run cost.
2. Was it a good brief?
Digest quality is a whole-brief score from 0 to 100. Think of it as a quick editorial health check.
85+ = strong brief 70-84 = decent brief below 70 = weak brief
3. If it was weak, why?
Open why this brief was strong or weak. You will see a plain-English summary first, then the top signals. The small score tags are only for debugging.
topic fit = relevance ranking = selection quality writeup = analysis quality freshness = novelty
